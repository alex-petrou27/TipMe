Overview

TipMe is a tipping application that lets a viewer send a tip to a content creator identified from a shared social media link or a handle. TipMe holds a balance on your behalf so that tips can be sent instantly. This policy explains what information TipMe collects, how it is used, and the choices available to you.

Information we collect

  • Your email address and a securely hashed password, used to create and sign in to your account.
  • Your balance and transaction records (deposits, tips sent and received, withdrawals), needed to run the service and to keep an accurate record.
  • If you register as a creator: your handle, the platform it belongs to, and the payout destination you provide.
  • The platform, username, and platform account ID for any social account you choose to connect (currently Instagram or TikTok), used solely to confirm that a handle belongs to you.
  • Basic device and diagnostic information needed to operate the app reliably.

TipMe does not read your posts, comments, messages, or content on any connected platform, and does not post or message on your behalf.

How we use information

  • To identify the correct recipient when a tip is sent.
  • To process deposits, tips and withdrawals through our third-party payment infrastructure providers.
  • To maintain a record of transactions for security and support purposes.

How tips are processed

TipMe is a custodial service: when you deposit funds, TipMe holds your balance and records it in your account until you send it or withdraw it. A tip is moved from your balance to the creator's balance (or, if the creator has not yet joined TipMe, held for them until they do). Deposits and withdrawals over the Bitcoin and Lightning networks are handled through third-party payment infrastructure providers.

Social account connections

When you connect a social account, we use that platform's official login process solely to verify which handle belongs to you. We do not store your access token after this check is complete. We do store the platform's permanent account ID and your handle at the time of connection.

Data retention and deletion

You may disconnect a linked social account at any time within the app. To request deletion of your TipMe account and associated data, contact us using the details below.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, or delete your personal data. Contact us to exercise these rights.
