Description of service

TipMe lets you hold a balance, deposit funds, and send a tip to a creator you identify through the app's sharing or handle features. Balances may be held in Bitcoin or in a US-dollar-pegged stablecoin, and are shown to you in the currency you choose. Deposits and withdrawals are facilitated through third-party payment infrastructure providers.

Custody of your balance

TipMe is a custodial service. Funds you deposit are held by TipMe on your behalf and are recorded as a balance in your account, which you can send to others or withdraw. A tip sent to a creator who has not yet joined TipMe is held until they join and link their handle, and can be reclaimed by you if it is still unclaimed.
